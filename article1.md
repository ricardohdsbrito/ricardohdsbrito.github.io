# From Metasploitable to $25k: My Bug Bounty Journey
## 🚀How I hacked Epic Games, Reddit, and Shopify without an OSCP.


### The Lab Rat (2019 - 2020)

> I’ve always been obsessed with how things break. In 2019, I finally stopped just reading about hacking and started doing it. I set up a home lab using VirtualBox—a classic Kali Linux box attacking a vulnerable Metasploitable machine.

I spent months recreating famous exploits like EternalBlue in my own isolated network. It felt like magic, but it was scripted. I wanted to know if I could find something new.

Then I found TryHackMe. I grinded those rooms relentlessly. Every night, every weekend. Eventually, I hit the Top 1% of users. I felt confident in my technical skills, but "real" hacking? That felt like a different beast entirely.

---

### The Leap of Faith (2021 - June 2022)

I looked at HackerOne but told myself I needed the OSCP first. I thought, "I need a certification to prove I’m legit." But I grew tired of the "tutorial hell." I created a HackerOne account in 2021, poked around, but wasn't serious. The impostor syndrome was real.

That changed in **June 2022**. I decided to stop preparing and start doing. I focused on Epic Games.
### 🛠️ The First Breakthrough
After days of poking, I found it. A vulnerability. I submitted the report, my heart pounding. A few days later, the bounty hit: **$5,000**.

I was ecstatic. It wasn't luck. It was validation.

---

### The COVID Lockdown & The Reddit Grind (Immediately after)

Right after the Epic Games win, COVID lockdowns hit. I noticed Reddit was running a promotion: extra bonuses on top of their bounties. This was the perfect storm.

&emsp;&emsp; **1.** `**The Info Leak**`: I found an informational disclosure first. Reddit paid me $100. It wasn't much, but it confirmed my first win wasn't a fluke.
&emsp;&emsp; **2.** `**The Low**`: A few days later, I found a low-impact issue. Got paid $600.
&emsp;&emsp; **3.** `**The Big One (The Handstand Push-up Story)**`: I found a section of the UI that looked susceptible to XSS. I tried everything. Nothing worked. I stepped away from my laptop, frustrated. I did some handstand push-ups to reset my brain (I was on a fitness kick). When I came back with fresh eyes, I saw the angle I was missing. Boom. I triggered the XSS. That bug paid **$6,000**.
&emsp;&emsp; **4.** `**The Cherry on Top**`: A bug I had submitted earlier that was triaged but unpaid finally came through. Another **$600**.

>   In one month, I made $12,300 from Epic Games and Reddit.I had quit my job? No. But I had bought my MacBook M1 with the earnings, telling myself this was just the beginning.

---

### 💡 Consistency & Diversification

After a short break, I hit Shopify for **$500**. Then came GitLab, then Grammarly.

I wasn't doing this full-time. I was in the middle of a PhD, working out, and living life. But HackerOne became my side hustle that actually worked.

---

### 🎯 Where I Am Now

Today, I have **24 vulnerabilities** across **5 different targets** (Epic Games, Reddit, Shopify, GitLab, and Grammarly) and have made **over $25,000**.

I now do pentesting as a contractor for a US company (they found me through my HackerOne profile), and I’m working on my certifications.

## 💡The Takeaway

You don't need the OSCP to start. You need the courage to stop reading and start clicking. You’ll fail, you’ll do handstand push-ups out of frustration, but eventually, you’ll find the bug.
